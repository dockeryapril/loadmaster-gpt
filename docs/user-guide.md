## Getting Started

### Account Creation & Authentication
1. **Visit the App:** Navigate to LoadMaster GPT in your web browser
2. **Sign Up:** Click "Sign Up" and enter your email address and a secure password
3. **Verify Email:** Check your email for a verification link and click it
4. **Sign In:** Return to the app and sign in with your credentials
5. **Business Setup:** Complete the initial business setup wizard (see [Business Setup](#business-setup))

### Business Setup Wizard
The app will guide you through essential business configuration:

**Required Information:**
- **Equipment Type:** Cargo Van, Straight Truck, or Hotshot
- **Revenue Structure:** How you get paid (percentage split, gross revenue, etc.)
- **Fixed Costs:** Weekly expenses (truck payments, insurance, etc.)
- **Fuel Settings:** Current fuel price and your truck's MPG
- **Operational Preferences:** Deadhead compensation, surcharges, etc.

**Quick Setup Templates:**
- Select from pre-configured templates for common trucking arrangements
- Templates automatically fill in industry-standard settings
- You can customize any settings after template selection

## Load Entry Methods

### 📸 OCR Screenshot Upload (Recommended)

**Supported Documents:**
- Load board screenshots (DAT, Truckstop, etc.)
- Broker emails and rate confirmations
- Mobile app screenshots
- PDF documents (as images)

**How to Use OCR:**
1. **Take Screenshot:** Capture the load information on your phone or computer
2. **Upload:** Click "Upload Screenshot" and select your image
3. **Review:** The app will extract fields automatically using AI
4. **Correct:** Edit any fields that weren't detected correctly
5. **Save:** Confirm the load details and save to your history

**OCR Tips for Best Results:**
- Ensure text is clear and readable
- Avoid blurry or low-resolution images
- Crop to show only the load information
- Good lighting improves accuracy
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP

**Weekly Upload Limits:**
- **Free Plan:** 4 OCR uploads per week (resets Sunday midnight)
- **Pro Plan:** 100 OCR uploads per week
- **Manual Entry:** Unlimited for all users

### 📝 Manual Entry

**Available for all users with no limits**

**Required Fields:**
- **Origin City:** Pickup location
- **Destination City:** Delivery location  
- **Miles:** Total distance (deadhead + loaded)
- **Rate:** Total pay for the load

**Optional Fields:**
- **FSC (Fuel Surcharge):** Additional fuel compensation
- **Weight:** Load weight in pounds
- **Load Number:** Reference number from broker/shipper
- **Accessorials:** Detention, lumper, layover, hazmat pay
- **Notes:** Personal notes about the load

## Load Calculator Features

### Core Calculations

**Revenue Per Mile (RPM):**
- **Gross RPM:** Total rate divided by total miles
- **Net Take-Home RPM (Pro):** After revenue split and business costs
- **Performance Indicators:** Color-coded based on your thresholds

**Profit Estimation:**
- **Fuel Cost:** Based on your MPG and current fuel price
- **Business Costs:** Fixed weekly costs converted to per-mile
- **Revenue Split:** Percentage going to company (if applicable)
- **Net Profit:** Estimated take-home after all costs

**Load Quality Indicators:**
- **Weight Impact:** How load weight affects fuel efficiency
- **Distance Analysis:** Short haul vs long haul considerations  
- **Accessorial Value:** Additional revenue opportunities

### Pro Features - Enhanced RPM Display

**Gross vs Net Breakdown:**
- **Gross RPM:** Before any deductions (`$2.15/mile`)
- **Net Take-Home RPM:** After revenue split and costs (`$1.85/mile`)
- **Business Impact:** Shows deductions per mile breakdown
- **Revenue Split:** Percentage and dollar impact
- **Fixed Costs:** Weekly costs converted to per-mile impact

## Troubleshooting

### OCR Issues

**Problem:** OCR not detecting fields correctly
**Solutions:**
1. **Image Quality:** Ensure screenshot is clear and well-lit
2. **Crop Image:** Remove unnecessary information around load details
3. **Text Size:** Make sure text is large enough to read
4. **File Format:** Use JPG or PNG format
5. **Manual Correction:** Edit fields after OCR processing

**Problem:** "Rate limit exceeded" error
**Solutions:**
1. **Check Usage:** View remaining uploads on dashboard
2. **Wait for Reset:** Weekly limits reset Sunday at midnight
3. **Use Manual Entry:** No limits on manual load entry
4. **Upgrade to Pro:** Get 100 uploads per week

### Authentication Issues

**Problem:** Can't sign in
**Solutions:**
1. **Check Email:** Verify your email address is correct
2. **Reset Password:** Use "Forgot Password" link
3. **Check Spam:** Email verification might be in spam folder
4. **Clear Browser:** Clear cache and cookies, try again

**Problem:** Verification email not received
**Solutions:**
1. **Check Spam Folder:** Email might be filtered
2. **Wait 5 Minutes:** Delivery can be delayed
3. **Resend Verification:** Use resend option if available
4. **Contact Support:** If issue persists

### Calculation Issues

**Problem:** RPM seems wrong
**Solutions:**
1. **Check Miles:** Verify total distance (deadhead + loaded)
2. **Check Rate:** Ensure total pay is entered correctly
3. **Review Settings:** Confirm fuel price and MPG are current
4. **Business Setup:** Verify revenue split and fixed costs

### Performance Issues

**Problem:** App loading slowly
**Solutions:**
1. **Check Internet:** Ensure stable connection
2. **Close Tabs:** Reduce browser memory usage
3. **Clear Cache:** Browser settings > Clear data
4. **Update Browser:** Use latest version
5. **Restart Device:** If on mobile, restart phone

### Debug Mode
**For detailed troubleshooting:**
1. Add `?debug=1` to the URL (e.g., `app.loadmastergpt.com/?debug=1`)
2. Reproduce the issue
3. Check browser console for error messages
4. Screenshot debug information for support

## Tips for Success

### Maximizing OCR Accuracy
1. **Consistent Screenshots:** Develop a routine for capturing load boards
2. **Good Lighting:** Natural light or bright room lighting works best  
3. **Steady Hands:** Avoid blurry images from camera shake
4. **Crop Appropriately:** Include only relevant load information
5. **Review Every Upload:** Always verify OCR detected fields correctly

### Effective Load Management
1. **Regular Updates:** Keep fuel prices and settings current
2. **Add Notes:** Record broker performance, route conditions, etc.
3. **Review Trends:** Use history to identify best lanes and brokers
4. **Set Realistic Thresholds:** Adjust RPM thresholds based on your market

### Business Performance
1. **Track Everything:** Record both good and bad loads for complete picture
2. **Weekly Reviews:** Analyze performance regularly
3. **Seasonal Adjustments:** Update thresholds for market changes
4. **Broker Relationships:** Use notes to track reliable brokers
